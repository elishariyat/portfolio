About

I am a civil engineering graduate working in exhibition booth design. My engineering background shapes how I plan structure, circulation and materials, so each design works as a real space and not only as an image.

I model each booth in SketchUp and produce the final renders in 3ds Max, using AutoCAD for drawings and Photoshop for finishing.
